Olympiad Strategy Tip

When to spot Nullstellensatz vs Pigeonhole: If a problem asks to prove the existence of elements under a modular prohibition (e.g., non-zero sum, distinct sub-graphs, or zero-sum subsequences), look for polynomial constructions where roots eliminate forbidden configurations. If degrees sum to strict cardinality bounds, Nullstellensatz beats standard extremal methods.
